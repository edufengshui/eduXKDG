# Purpose guides

For each Purpose, the conditions that make a date / hour qualify. Opens from the 📄 icon
next to the Purpose. Two parts: **Date (XKDG)** and **Feng Shui (QMDJ)**.

Legend — *Structure* = the required XKDG / blood-link configuration · *Especially favoured* =
spirits this goal weights extra · *Timely formations* = special Qimen structures that also flag the date.

> **General Feng Shui rule (every door below):** a door only counts when it sits on a favourable
> structure — **San Qi 三奇 (乙丙丁)** or **Wu 戊** on a good door.

> **Commander 值符 (QMDJ):** wherever it sits, it boosts effectiveness for **every** purpose.

> **Shared favourable spirits (count toward every Date, incl. Wealth):**
> **Cerulean Dragon 青龍**, **Golden Box 金匱**, **Heaven Virtue 天德**, **Fate Master 司命**, **Lu 祿**.
> The "Especially favoured" list under each Purpose is what that goal weights *extra* (on top of these).

> **Flying Stars (玄空) booster:** star **9** helps **every** purpose. Each purpose also has its own
> lucky flying stars (below); a lucky **山 mountain** or **向 facing** star at the activation palace adds effectiveness.

## 🏥 Health

**Date (XKDG)**
- Structure: a **Parent (印 Resource)** present in the date (best on the **day pillar**).
- **Celestial Doctor 天醫 (Tian Yi)** — the healing star: the date's hour branch matches the day stem's Heavenly Doctor, or the date's day branch matches the person's Heavenly Doctor. *(This is the 天醫 healing star, not the Noble 天乙貴人.)*
- Especially favoured (extra weight): **Cerulean Dragon 青龍**, **Jade Hall 玉堂**.
- Timely formations: **Deity Dun 神遁**, **Human Dun 人遁**.

**Feng Shui (QMDJ)**
- Door: **Rest 休 (Xiu)**.
- QMDJ star: **Heart 天心 (Xin)**.
- Flying Stars: **8** (and 9).

## 💼 Career

**Date (XKDG)**
- Structure: a **Parent (印 Resource)** present (best on the **day pillar**) **and** a **Noble 貴人**.
- Especially favoured (extra weight): **Bright Hall 明堂**, **Fate Master 司命**, **Lu 祿**.
- Timely formations: **Heaven Dun 天遁**, **Wind Dun 風遁**, **Dragon Dun 龍遁**, **Cloud Dun 云遁**.

**Feng Shui (QMDJ)**
- Door: **Open 開 (Kai)** or **View 景 (JingS)**.
- QMDJ stars: **Hero 天英 (Ying)**, **Official 天任 (Ren)**.
- QMDJ spirit: **Norm 勾陳**.
- Flying Stars: **6** (and 9).

## 💰 Wealth

**Date (XKDG)**
- Structure: **day pillar in the Child role + a Parent present** (Child + Parent).
- Wealth ✦ (controlling / 克 relationships): person's day stem controls the date's day stem; the date's day stem controls the hour / month / year stems; the same on the Nayin (Qi) level. Each adds a wealth point.
- Especially favoured (extra weight): **Golden Box 金匱**, **Cerulean Dragon 青龍**, **Jade Hall 玉堂**, **Lu 祿**.
- Timely formations: **Earth Dun 地遁**, **Cloud Dun 云遁**, **Rest Pretenses 休詐**, **Earth Borrows 地假**.

**Feng Shui (QMDJ)**
- Door: **Birth 生 (Sheng)**.
- Bonus: **Wu 戊** adds a wealth bonus; strongest when reinforced by **Wu 戊 / Bing 丙**.
- Flying Stars: **9**.
